From 2023 to 2026 at AiBall, I developed and optimized production computer-vision systems used to
record, process, and analyze padel matches.

## Core Contributions

- Developed player and ball detection and tracking across multiple cameras.
- Built 3D ball-trajectory reconstruction and improved tracking accuracy and runtime efficiency.
- Contributed to a distributed video-processing platform using cloud storage, background workers, and automated alerts.

## Product and Operations

- Designed backend models and APIs for clubs, courts, cameras, reservations, players, and matches.
- Integrated club-management and reservation platforms, normalizing booking data and automating match creation.
- Built dashboards and monitoring for data quality, processing pipelines, camera/NVR health, and club performance.
